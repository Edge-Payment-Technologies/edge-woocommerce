This is the official Wordpress plugin for utilizing Edge Payment Technologies, Inc. as a payment gateway in WooCommerce stores.

== Description ==

Edge Payments for WooCommerce makes it easy to accept and manage online card payments directly from your WooCommerce store.

Built for businesses that need reliable payment infrastructure and responsive support, the Edge plugin connects your store to the Edge payments platform without disrupting your existing WooCommerce workflow.

Payments built for your WooCommerce store

With Edge Payments, you can:

* **Accept major credit and debit cards directly** through your WooCommerce checkout.
* **Keep checkout simple for customers** with a payment experience designed to work smoothly across desktop and mobile devices.
* **Protect sensitive card data** using secure hosted payment fields and tokenization, so card information does not pass through your WooCommerce server.
* **Add an extra layer of transaction security** with support for 3D Secure authentication.
* **Issue refunds from your existing workflow** while keeping payment activity connected to the corresponding WooCommerce order.
* **Manage payments through Edge** with access to transaction details, refunds, disputes, and other payment activity through your Edge account.
* **Work with a payments provider built for complex businesses.** Edge supports a broad range of business models, including merchants that may require more specialized underwriting or payment support.
* **Get hands-on support when you need it.** Our team works directly with merchants throughout onboarding and ongoing payment operations.

Edge Payments is designed to give WooCommerce merchants straightforward payment processing without unnecessary complexity.

Connect your store, start accepting payments, and manage your payment activity with Edge.
